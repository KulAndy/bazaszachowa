### Possible search parameters

- **Player**

  You must enter at least one player's last name.

- **ECO codes**

  [ECO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopaedia_of_Chess_Openings)

- **Database**

  - **Poland** — tournaments mainly from Poland, ~700k games, faster database.
  - **All** — all games, ~16.5M games, slower database.

- **Search** — case insensitive

  - **Classic** — works with both `Nowak, Jan` and `Nowak, J`. Wildcards are allowed (`_` = one character, `%` = any sequence of characters).
  - **Exact** — only matches exact input like `Nowak, Jan`. Faster and recommended if you know the full name.
