Anyone interested can upload missing games to the database. Just send them using this form.

After verification, the games will be added to the database.

I would also appreciate email contact if someone would like to share printed materials, e.g. tournament bulletins.

### Please note

- Games without a year will be removed.
- The full name should be provided in the same order as on the FIDE website, unless the player is not registered as `Lastname, Firstname`.
- National characters will be converted to the English alphabet.
- Variants such as Chess960 will not be accepted.
- Games with fewer than 4 moves will be removed.
